CamStream streams your Android phone's camera to your computer as a webcam, over the
same TCP protocol used by the official DroidCam app. It is a from-scratch, ad-free,
open-source replacement for that app's phone side, and works unmodified with the
existing open-source droidcam-linux-client and v4l2loopback-dc pipeline already
available on the desktop.

No DroidCam code is included; the wire protocol was independently recovered by reading
droidcam-linux-client's own GPL-2.0 source, published specifically so people can build
interoperable clients. See PROTOCOL.md in the source repository for the full writeup.

Features:

* CameraX-based capture, JPEG-encoded, streamed over the DroidCam-compatible TCP
  protocol on port 4747
* Foreground service that keeps streaming while the screen is off or the app is
  backgrounded
* Front/back camera switching mid-stream, without dropping the PC connection
* Minimal, modern UI: connection status, the address to enter on the PC side, and
  start/stop/switch controls. Deliberately no camera preview in the UI itself, since
  rendering one would cost battery for no benefit
* Works with any client that speaks the protocol, including droidcam-linux-client and
  the resulting v4l2loopback device in any video call app

Requires a computer running droidcam-linux-client and its v4l2loopback-dc kernel
module; both devices must be on the same local network.
